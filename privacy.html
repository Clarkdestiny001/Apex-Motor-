Privacy Policy

We take the same care with your personal information that we take with every vehicle that leaves our showroom. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and how it's protected.

01

Information We Collect

Apex Motors collects information you provide directly to us, information gathered automatically as you use our site, and information received from trusted third parties such as financing and insurance partners.

Information you provide directly includes your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any details you share when requesting a test drive, submitting a finance pre-qualification, valuing a trade-in, or contacting our concierge team. If you finance a vehicle through Apex, our lending partners may also collect income and employment information as part of that separate application process.

Information collected automatically includes your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring URLs, and general location data derived from your IP address, gathered through cookies and similar technologies described in our Cookie Policy.

  • Contact details submitted through forms (name, email, phone)
  • Vehicle and appointment preferences (models viewed, test drive requests)
  • Device and usage data (browser type, session duration, pages visited)
  • Communications you send us, including call recordings for quality assurance where legally permitted

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate the dealership, serve your requests, and improve the experience of shopping with Apex Motors. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Schedule and confirm test drives and service appointments
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form or concierge line
  • Process trade-in valuations and connect you with financing partners you choose to engage
  • Send transactional messages, such as appointment confirmations and delivery updates
  • With your consent, send marketing communications about new arrivals and private previews
  • Analyze site usage to improve inventory presentation and overall performance
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

03

Data Security

We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmitted through our site is encrypted in transit via TLS, and access to customer records is restricted to personnel who need it to perform their role.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

04

Cookies

Apex Motors uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep the site functional, understand how visitors use our inventory tools, and, where you've given consent, personalize marketing. This includes essential cookies required for the site to operate, analytics cookies that help us understand traffic patterns, and marketing cookies used to tailor offers.

For a full breakdown of the categories we use and how to manage your preferences, see our Cookie Policy.

05

Third-Party Services

We share information with trusted third parties only where necessary to provide our services:

  • Financing and leasing partners, when you submit a finance or lease application
  • Insurance providers, when you request an introduction through our Insurance Support service
  • Transport and logistics providers, to arrange nationwide vehicle delivery
  • Analytics providers, to help us understand site performance in aggregate
  • Service providers who host our website, process payments, or support our customer communications

These partners are contractually required to use your information only for the purposes we've authorized and to protect it with reasonable safeguards. We do not permit third parties to use your information for their own independent marketing without your consent.

06

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing, including marketing communications.

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any message
  • Request a summary of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal and contractual record-keeping requirements

To exercise any of these rights, contact our privacy team using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
