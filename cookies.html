01

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow a site to recognize your browser, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used across visits. Apex Motors uses cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage, to support core site functionality and to help us understand and improve the experience of browsing our inventory.

02

Types of Cookies We Use

We use four broad categories of cookies on this site. Strictly necessary cookies are always active, as the site cannot function properly without them; the remaining categories are used only where permitted and, where required by law, only with your consent.

  • Strictly Necessary — required for core functionality such as navigation, form submission, and security
  • Analytics — help us understand how visitors interact with the site in aggregate
  • Functional — remember choices you make, like filter selections or comparison picks
  • Marketing — used to tailor offers and measure the performance of our campaigns

03

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand which vehicles, pages, and features visitors engage with most, how long sessions typically last, and where visitors encounter friction. This information is used in aggregate to improve site performance and inventory presentation — it is not used to build individual profiles for advertising purposes.

You can opt out of analytics cookies without affecting your ability to browse inventory or use core site features.

04

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember choices you've made while browsing, such as active inventory filters, vehicles selected for comparison, or your progress through a multi-step form like our finance calculator. Disabling these cookies means the site may not remember your preferences between page loads.

05

Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies are used, with your consent, to tailor communications about new arrivals, private previews, and promotions to your interests, and to measure how effective those campaigns are. These cookies may be set by us or by advertising partners we work with. Declining marketing cookies means you may still see Apex Motors advertising, but it will be less tailored to your browsing activity.

06

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time — most browsers allow you to block cookies entirely, block third-party cookies only, or clear cookies already stored on your device. Because blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the site from functioning correctly, we recommend managing optional categories individually where your browser supports it.

  • Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge each offer cookie controls under their privacy or security settings
  • Opting out of one browser or device does not opt you out on others — preferences must be set per browser
  • Clearing cookies will reset saved filters, comparisons, and any dismissed on-site notices

For more on how the information gathered through cookies is used, see our Privacy Policy.
